== Overview

ACME Financial Services is a bank that runs customer-facing workloads on OpenShift. A small platform team keeps the cluster healthy, helps application teams ship, and takes turns on the incident queue.

You are Sarah, a senior platform engineer at ACME. Morgan, from information security, is the person who pages you when something looks wrong.

== Why it matters

The rest of this track assumes you can open the consoles and see the two namespaces before the incident starts. Checking that now keeps the later exercises about the incident, not about login.

== Your mission

Confirm you can open this cluster and see `payment-gateway-{workshop_user_suffix}` and `vulnerable-workload-{workshop_user_suffix}`.

=== Prerequisites

* The roadshow home page setup script has been run (`setup/lab-environment.sh`)
* Console URLs and passwords from the xref:ROOT:index.adoc[home page]

.Sign in to the OpenShift console at {openshift_cluster_console_url} as {openshift_cluster_admin_username} with the password on the home page
+
. Open the *OpenShift Console* tab, or go to `{openshift_cluster_console_url}`.
. Sign in with `{openshift_cluster_admin_username}` and the password on the home page.

image::fsi-security/acme-cluster.png[OpenShift console,link=self, window=blank, width=100%]

.Run oc whoami, then list pods in payment-gateway-{workshop_user_suffix} and vulnerable-workload-{workshop_user_suffix}. Expect running pods in both
+
[source,bash,subs="+macros,+attributes",role=execute]
----
oc whoami
oc get pods -n payment-gateway-{workshop_user_suffix}
oc get pods -n vulnerable-workload-{workshop_user_suffix}
----
+
`payment-gateway-{workshop_user_suffix}` has one `payment-gateway` pod and an `httpd` container.
+
`vulnerable-workload-{workshop_user_suffix}` has three pods: `message-broker`, `socketio-server`, and `edge-server`.
+
If a namespace is missing, sync Application `security-response` in OpenShift GitOps. An instructor can also run `./setup/deploy-incident-env.sh` for more than one attendee suffix.

.Sign in to RHACS at {acs_route} as {acs_portal_username} with the password on the home page
+
. Open the *Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security* tab, or go to `{acs_route}[{acs_route}^]`.
. Sign in with `{acs_portal_username}` and the password on the home page.
